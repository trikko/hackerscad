Measure

Objects and types

Solid
A 3D body: boxes, cylinders, booleans, features. Every operation gives a new one.
Profile
A flat 2D outline on XY (rect, circle, text, sketch.profile; a shape's profile is a one-shape sketch with everything given): combine with + - &, extrude or revolve it into a Solid.
Path
A curve in space (path, spline, helix) to sweep a profile along.
Vec
A 2D or 3D vector or point: vec(10.mm, 5.mm, 0.mm); .x .y .z .length.
Vec2
A 2D vector.
Vec3
A 3D vector.
Direction
An axis direction: X, Y, Z, -X…, or any vector.
Plane
An infinite plane: an origin and a normal (XY, plane(…), body.plane(faces.top)); cuts, mirrors, sections.
Axis
A line in space: an origin and a direction (axis(…), body.axis(edges…)); rotations and circular patterns.
Frame
A coordinate system on a face or plane: origin, X, Y and Z (body.frame(faces.top)); placeOn builds on it.
BBox
A solid's bounding box: min, max, size, center.
Halves
What halves gives: .above (where the plane's normal points) and .below.
Edges
A selection of a solid's edges, for fillet and chamfer: edges.parallel(Z), edges.of(faces.top)…
Faces
A selection of a solid's faces, for shell, draft, frames: faces.top, faces.normal(X)…
Edge
One edge, inside a selector predicate: edges.filter(e => e.isCircle && e.radius == 3.mm).
Face
One face, inside a selector predicate: faces.filter(f => f.center.z > 10.mm).
Sketch
A constrained 2D drawing: points, lines, arcs, circles, ellipses and constraints; the solver places them, .profile gives the outline.
Drawing
A technical sheet (views, sections, dimensions) returned by a @drawing function; exported as PDF, SVG, DXF.
Assembly
Parts placed by their connections (planned).
Color
A colour: "steelblue", "#RRGGBB", "#RRGGBBAA" or rgb(…).
Length
A length with its unit: 10.mm, 2.5.cm; bare numbers given to geometry are mm.
Angle
An angle: 30.deg, 0.5.rad.
Area
An area (Length × Length).
Volume
A volume.
bool
true or false.
int
An integer.
double
A number.
float
A number.
real
A number.
long
An integer.
string
Text.

On top of another solid.

Aligns bounding boxes per axis: "min", "center" or "max".
s.linearPattern(n: int, step: Vec) → Solid
n copies, step apart, as one solid.

Cuts with a plane into the pieces it leaves, both sides, along X, then Y, then Z: for @parts, or .above(plane), .largest…
s.halves(plane: Plane) → Halves
Cuts with a plane in two sides: .above (where the normal points) and .below, each one solid.

Profiles (2D)

rect(w: Length, h: Length, p: Vec, q: Vec, center: Vec, angle: Angle, corner: Length) → Profile
A rectangle: centred on the origin (or center:), from two opposite corners, or from one corner and its size; angle: turns it about its centre (a rotated, tilted rectangle, at an angle), corner: rounds its corners. s.rect with everything given.
circle(r: Length, d: Length, center: Vec) → Profile
A circle centred on the origin, or round a point. s.circle with everything given.
ellipse(rx: Length, ry: Length, angle: Angle, center: Vec) → Profile
An ellipse centred on the origin (or round a point), rx along X, ry along Y; angle: turns it. s.ellipse with everything given.
polygon(points: Vec[], smooth: bool, n: int, r | d: Length, fit: string, angle: Angle, center: Vec) → Profile
A polygon through the points (smooth: true, a smooth closed curve through them instead); or a regular one of n sides, centred on the origin or round a point: d/r the circle through its corners, or with fit: "sides" the circle its sides touch; angle: the direction of its first corner, else its first side lies at the bottom, horizontal. s.polygon with everything given.
slot(length: Length, w: Length, p: Vec, q: Vec, center: Vec, angle: Angle) → Profile
A stadium: length end to end along X, centred on the origin (or center:, angle: turned); or round two centres p and q, w wide. s.slot with everything given.
teardrop(r | d: Length, angle: Angle, flat: bool, center: Vec) → Profile
A hole that prints sideways without supports: a circle with a 45° point towards +Y (up on a wall); angle: another point (3° to 90° from the vertical), flat: true cuts the point. s.teardrop with everything given.
text(text: string, size: Length, font: string) → Profile
A text outline starting at the origin on the baseline.
p.round(r: Length) → Profile
Rounds every corner, outward and inward, holes too; an error names a corner r is too large for.
p.offset(distance: Length) → Profile
Grows (d > 0) or shrinks the outline; on a plane, the parallel plane.
p.across(axis: X | Y) → Profile
The profile stood up across X or Y, as extrude(along:) stands it (across X: its x along Y, its y up Z), at the origin: move it along the axis and loft through several, or extrude it where it is.
s.offset(distance: Length) → Solid
Grows (d > 0) or shrinks the solid, round where it turns (its walls thickened).
p.extrude(height: Length, taper: Angle, symmetric: bool, twist: Angle, along: X | Y) → Solid
A solid along +Z from the profile (a negative height: down, towards -Z); taper: 3.deg leans its sides in going away from the profile, whichever way the solid goes (the outline narrows, holes widen; a negative taper widens); twist: 90.deg turns it around Z as it rises, evenly. along: X stands the profile up across X (its x along Y, its y up Z) and pushes it towards +X, along: Y (its x along X) towards +Y; a negative height towards -X or -Y. A profile lying in another plane (a section, one turned or drawn on a face) is extruded across its own plane, from where it is.
p.revolve(axis: Axis, angle: Angle) → Solid
Turns the profile around an axis in its plane, on one side of it (touching it at most), by an angle up to 360°; without one, x is the radius and y the height along Z.
p.sweep(path: Path, frenet: bool, twist: Angle) → Solid
Moves the profile along a path; twist: 360.deg turns it once around the path on the way.
loft(profiles: Profile[], ruled: bool, inside: Vec3[][]) → Solid
A solid through profiles placed in space; or through rings of points (Vec3[][], the same count each): one smooth surface closed round them, much faster for many rings; inside: rings for an inner wall, hollow with a floor, open between the last rings (which lie in one plane).
hull(shapes…) → Solid
The smallest convex shape around solids and profiles: hull(a, b) or hull([a, b]); profiles in one plane give a profile.
minkowski(a, b) → Solid
a moved by every point of b: minkowski(box(…), sphere(2.mm)) rounds the box; one of the two must be convex.
s.cut(profile: Profile, on: Frame, depth: Length, through: bool, taper: Angle) → Solid
Digs a profile into a face: body.cut(p, on: frame, depth: 2.mm).
s.thread(face: Faces, size: string, pitch: Length, length: Length, turns: number, starts: int, left: bool, clearance: Length, chamfer: bool, flip: bool) → Solid
A thread on a cylindrical face, outside a shaft or inside a hole (made at the tap drill size): "M8", "M8x1", "1/4-20 UNC", "#10-32 UNF", "Tr12x3", "G1/2", tapered "R1/2" and "1/2 NPT".
s.extrude(faces: Selector, depth: Length, along: Vec3) → Solid
Pulls flat faces of the solid outwards: body.extrude(faces.top, depth: 5.mm); along: slants the sweep; body.cut(faces.top, depth: 2.mm) pushes them in.

Sketches

sketch(on: Frame | Plane, origin: Vec, x: Direction) → Sketch
A constrained 2D sketch: add points, lines, constraints, then take .profile. On XY, or on a face of a solid (its edges as references, its axes as frame's) or a frame or plane: the profile then lies there.
sk.edges → Entity[]
A sketch on a face: the face's edges as references (fixed construction lines, arcs, circles), each loop from its lowest edge counter-clockwise, as a rect's sides: bottom, right, top, left; the outside first.
sk.origin → Point
A sketch on a face or plane: its origin (the face's centre) as a fixed reference point.
sk.project(edges: Edges) → Entity[]
Edges of the solid the sketch is on, flattened onto its plane as references: a circle seen aslant is an ellipse, other curves lines along them.
sk.point(x: Length, y: Length, fixed: bool, construction: bool) → Point
A point where it starts; constraints move it.
sk.line(p: Point, q: Point, construction: bool) → Line
A line between two points; construction: true (on every geometry call and shape) makes a guide to constrain to, drawn dashed, never part of the profile.
sk.arc(center: Point, start: Point, end: Point, construction: bool) → Arc
An arc, counter-clockwise.
sk.polyline(points, closed: bool, construction: bool) → Line[]
Lines through points (vectors become new points).
sk.spline(points, closed: bool) → SketchSpline
A smooth curve through points (vectors become new points): open, its ends join lines and arcs as theirs do; closed: true, a loop of its own. Its points move it: constrain them.
sk.circle(center: Point, r: Length, d: Length) → Circle
A circle round a point (a new one at the origin without it); without r/d the radius is left to the constraints. The same shape as the profile circle(…).
sk.ellipse(center: Point, rx: Length, ry: Length, angle: Angle) → Ellipse
An ellipse: rx along its own x axis, angle from X, ry across; what is not given is left to the constraints. The same shape as the profile ellipse(…).
sk.rect(p: Point, q: Point, center: Point, w: Length, h: Length, angle: Angle | "free", corner: Length) → Line[]
A rectangle: its 4 lines (bottom, right, top, left), horizontal and vertical; angle: turns it (its first side from X), "free" leaves the turn to the constraints; corner: rounds them. The same shape as the profile rect(…).
sk.polygon(center: Point, n: int, r: Length, d: Length, fit: string, angle: Angle) → Line[]
A regular polygon: its n lines, corners on a construction circle of the size given; fit: "sides" makes its sides touch it instead; angle: the direction of the first corner, else the turn is left free (starting with the first side at the bottom, horizontal). s.polygon([p1, p2, p3]): the closed polyline through the points. The same shapes as the profile polygon(…).
sk.slot(p: Point, q: Point, w: Length, length: Length, center: Point, angle: Angle) → [Line, Arc, Line, Arc]
Two half circles round p and q joined by tangent lines: [line, arc round q, line, arc round p]; or by its length end to end, along X (angle: turned) round center: or a new point at the origin. The same shape as the profile slot(…).
